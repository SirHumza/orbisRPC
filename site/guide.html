How to show your PS4 game on Discord — with no PC

orbisRPC puts the game you're playing, its cover art and an elapsed play timer on your Discord profile, posted straight from a jailbroken PS4. There is no PC, script or cable in the loop.

Download orbisRPC View on GitHub

What you need

  • A jailbroken PS4 on firmware 5.05–13.52 with GoldHEN running
  • A payload loader (elfldr on port 9021, or GoldHEN BinLoader on 9090) — or just the setup PKG
  • A Discord account and your user session token
  • Internet on the PS4 that can reach Discord

Install in five minutes

  1. Install the PKG. Put orbisRPC Setup on the PS4 and open it from the home screen.
  2. Choose INSTALL / UPDATE. It copies the payload into /data/payloads/ and writes your config.
  3. Enter your Discord token when asked. (This is the #1 setup issue — the token guide walks through it.)
  4. Enable auto-run. Let it add orbisRPC to the GoldHEN auto-run queue so it starts on every boot.
  5. Restart and play. Launch a game and watch your Discord profile update with the game, cover art and timer.

Why not the official integration or a PC script?

Discord's official PlayStation link shows a basic PSN status you can't control, and it gets overridden by local activity. The older community tools (PS4-Rich-Presence-for-Discord, playstation-discord-status) are PC scripts that need a computer running while you play. orbisRPC is the one that runs on the console and needs no PC.

Common questions

How do I show what I'm playing on PS4 on Discord?

Install orbisRPC on a jailbroken PS4. It posts the current game, cover art and play timer to Discord automatically — no PC needed.

Why doesn't Discord show my PS4 game?

The official PlayStation integration only shows a basic PSN status. For the exact game with cover art and a timer from a jailbroken PS4, use orbisRPC.

Do I need a PC for PS4 Discord Rich Presence?

Not with orbisRPC. It runs entirely on the console. Other tools require a PC script running while you play.

Does it work on a non-jailbroken PS4?

No — orbisRPC is a payload and requires GoldHEN.
